3D Part Print Data Retention Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how long the data used by 3D Part Print is kept and how it is deleted. It should be read together with the 3D Part Print Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

3D Part Print keeps data on your device only. The developer does not operate a server that stores your personal information, so there is no developer-side retention period to manage. Data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

2. Data and how long it is kept

DataWhere it is keptHow long it is kept
Selected model and yearMemory while the app is openCleared when the app closes
Unlimited access statusSaved on the deviceUntil the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled
Downloaded STL and 3MF filesCreated on the device and handed to the share menu or browserNot kept by 3D Part Print. Any saved copy is managed by you or the app you share it with
Purchase recordsGoogle Play or the App StoreUnder the retention rules of the store

3. Deleting data

  • Remove all app data: uninstall 3D Part Print or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • Restore access after reinstalling: use Restore purchases in the app. Purchase history stays with the store, so access can be restored without keeping any data in 3D Part Print.
  • Delete a downloaded file: remove it from the location where you saved it or shared it.

4. Developer records

3D Part Print does not create user accounts and does not hold personal records about users. If the app later adds accounts, reports, or cloud storage, this policy will be updated first to state the retention period for each kind of data and how it is deleted on request.

5. Requests

To ask about any data related to JeepPrint, contact the developer at the email address listed on the 3D Part Print store page.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows when it was last revised.
