3D Part Print Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

3D Part Print helps Jeep owners find commonly failing parts for their vehicle and download 3D-printable replacement files (STL and 3MF). This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does not collect.

1. Information the app does not collect

3D Part Print does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or location, and does not collect personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Vehicle selection: the model and year you choose are kept while the app is open so that the parts list and part pages stay consistent. They are not sent anywhere.
  • Unlock status: after you buy unlimited access, the app saves a flag on your device so the unlocked parts remain available when you reopen the app.
  • Downloaded files: STL and 3MF files are created on your device when you request them. They are shared through your device's share menu or saved by your browser. The app does not keep a copy.

3. Purchases

Unlimited access is sold as a one-time purchase through Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad). Payment is handled entirely by the store. 3D Part Print never sees your payment details. The store provides the app with confirmation that the purchase was made and may share purchase information with the app as described in its own privacy policy. Google Play is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the App Store by the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Backend service

The app is connected to Supabase, a hosted backend service. At present no personal information is sent to it by the app, and no screen in the current version uses it to store user data. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

5. Data sharing

3D Part Print does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Information is shared only where you choose to share a downloaded file, or where a store processes a purchase as described above.

6. Children

3D Part Print is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Data retention

The data described in Section 2 remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Purchase records are kept by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies. For full details, see the 3D Part Print Data Retention Policy.

8. Your choices

You can remove all data stored by 3D Part Print by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your device settings. You can restore a previous purchase at any time using the Restore purchases option in the app.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and the date above will be revised.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer at the email address listed on the 3D Part Print store page.
